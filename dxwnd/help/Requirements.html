Minimum requirements

The following requirements ensure DxWnd works properly with the minimal functionalities:

OS: Microsoft Windows XP Service Pack 1 or newer

CPU: CPU with SSE2 support

Video hardware: Video card with support for 16-bit colors (Desktop color depth must be set to 16-bit or higher)

Sound hardware: Sound card with support for PCM audio


Recommended requirements

For utilising most of what DxWnd offers, the following changes in requirements are needed:

OS: Microsoft Windows Vista or better

Video driver: The video driver must be WDDM compliant.
